Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

Catalyst Network Security LLC (“we”, “us”) operates the ExamForge mobile application (iOS and Android). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect location data, contacts, camera access, or any other sensitive information beyond the above.

2. Sign in with Apple / Google

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide a private relay email address (@privaterelay.appleid.com) instead of your real email. If you choose Sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and name. We store and use only what the provider shares and never attempt to resolve or de-anonymise relay addresses.

3. Guest Mode

Using the app as a guest requires no account. No personal data is collected or transmitted. Quiz progress in guest mode is stored locally on your device only.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting (US infrastructure). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time (see below). Purchase transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by applicable financial regulations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA (California), or similar laws, including:

  • Access to the data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your account and associated data
  • Data portability
  • Objection to processing

To exercise any of these rights, email examforge@carbonnetworksecurity.com.

7. Children

ExamForge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance. The “last updated” date above always reflects the current version.
